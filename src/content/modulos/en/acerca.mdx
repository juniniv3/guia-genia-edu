## Purpose

The **Guía GenIA Edu** guides teachers in using generative artificial intelligence to create **adaptive teaching materials**, with sound pedagogical, ethical and responsible judgment.

It responds to a problem identified in the literature: teachers have more and more AI tools available, but **little practical guidance** for integrating them with pedagogical intent. In many cases they use AI intuitively or experimentally, without guidelines that ensure the relevance and quality of the content.

## Foundations

The guide draws on three groups of references:

- **Generative AI in education.** Its teaching value isn't automatic: it depends on the mediation of a trained teacher (Holmes et al., 2019; UNESCO, 2024).
- **Teachers' digital competencies.** A progression that goes from general teacher digital competence (DigCompEdu) to AI literacy (UNESCO), and from there to **prompt engineering as reflective teaching practice** (Federiakin et al., 2024; Lo, 2023; White et al., 2023; Dourvas et al., 2026).
- **Adaptive teaching materials.** Learning principles: scaffolding (Vygotsky), meaningful learning (Ausubel) and connectivism (Siemens). Design criteria: differentiated instruction (Tomlinson) and Universal Design for Learning (CAST). The teacher is seen as a designer (Leong et al., 2022).

## Origin

This guide is the main product of the master's thesis *"Guía pedagógica para el uso de IA generativa en la creación de materiales adaptativos: una experiencia en la Maestría en Educación UNAB"* (A teaching guide for using generative AI to create adaptive materials: an experience in the UNAB Master's in Education), developed in the School of Social Sciences, Arts and Humanities at the **Universidad Autónoma de Bucaramanga**, Colombia.

- **Author:** Luis Jesús Flórez Moreno
- **Advisor:** Laura Milena Palacios Mora, PhD

The research follows an **action research** design: the guide is designed from a review of the literature, shared with students of the Master's in Education and improved based on their perceptions of its clarity, relevance, applicability and ethical guidance.

<Callout type="idea" title="Base version">
This is a **base version** of the guide. It will be adjusted in each research cycle with input from the teachers who use it.
</Callout>

<Callout type="consejo" title="About this translation">
